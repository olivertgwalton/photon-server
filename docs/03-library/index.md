---
title: "Build your library"
summary: "Turn folders of films and shows into a library, with descriptions and pictures, that stays up to date."
---

A library is a folder of films, shows or both that photon-server reads. It looks at each file's
name to work out what it is, then looks the title up online to fetch its description, cast and
pictures. It keeps an eye on the folder, so what you add, rename or remove shows up on its own.

This section takes you from a folder of files to a library that looks the way you want.
