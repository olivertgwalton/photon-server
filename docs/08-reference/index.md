These pages are for looking things up rather than reading through: what the binary reads from its
environment and the disk, every part of the Compose file, every route of the API, how the server
compares with others, and what its refusals and log messages mean.

## Pages

- [Configuration](01-configuration.md): environment variables, subcommands, exit codes, ports,
  folders, tools and logs.
- [Docker Compose reference](02-docker-compose.md): each variable, service and volume of the
  Compose file, and what to add to it.
- [API reference](03-api.md): every route, generated from the server's own description.
- [Photon beside Jellyfin, Plex and silo](04-performance.md): latency, throughput, memory and CPU,
  measured on one machine.
- [Fix playback and connection problems](05-troubleshooting.md): the server's refusals and log
  messages, and what to do about each.
