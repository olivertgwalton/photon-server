---
title: "Maintain your server"
summary: "Keep photon-server backed up, up to date and healthy, and see what it does by itself."
---

photon-server looks after most of itself. It backs up its database every three days, scans your libraries, retries work that failed, and clears away what is no longer needed. What is left to you is checking it is doing so, putting it back if something goes wrong, and updating it. Everything here is under Settings › Server, for an admin.
