Everyone who watches has a **profile**. A profile keeps that person's progress, favourites and
playlists, and says what they are allowed to see.

A device signs in as a profile in one of three ways:

- with the profile's name and password;
- through a sign-in service your household already uses, such as Authelia or Pocket ID;
- on a television, by a short code you approve from your phone or computer.

Each device stays signed in on its own. You can sign any one of them out without touching the
rest.
